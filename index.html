Channels Uploader

Channels Uploader is a private tool used by one creator to publish their own short educational and entertainment videos to their own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.

The channels

Fact Odyssey — science, space and history facts in under 60 seconds. youtube.com/@factodyssey60
Tundra & Tide — nature and wildlife shorts with a fact in every clip. youtube.com/@tundraandtide
Viral Voltage — viral moments clipped from creators who allow clipping. youtube.com/@viralvoltagehq

What the tool does

  • Uploads videos the owner has made to the owner's own YouTube channels, with a title, description and tags.
  • Reads basic channel information to confirm it is uploading to the correct channel.
  • It does not read, change or delete other videos, comments, subscribers or any other account data.
