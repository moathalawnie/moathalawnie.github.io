Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Channels Uploader ("the app") is a private tool used only by its owner to upload their own videos to their own YouTube channels (Fact Odyssey, Tundra & Tide and Viral Voltage). It is not offered to other users.

Data the app accesses

  • YouTube upload permission (youtube.upload) — to upload the owner's videos.
  • Read-only channel information (youtube.readonly) — only to confirm the video is going to the correct channel (channel ID and title).

How data is used and stored

  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are used only to upload the owner's videos.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • The app does not collect data about viewers or any other YouTube users.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops the app immediately; locally stored tokens are deleted by the owner.
